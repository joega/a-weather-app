# A Weather App 0.70.1

This patch makes Live desktop match the city you are viewing and simplifies
starting effects on the right monitor.

- **Live desktop follows the viewed city.** Switching cities updates desktop
  effects to that city's weather. Its forecast continues to refresh while the
  app window is hidden and effects are running.
- **Automatic monitor selection.** Starting effects defaults to the monitor
  containing the weather app window, rather than another focused window.
  Moving the app before starting uses its new monitor.
- **Explicit monitor choice remains available.** Settings lets you choose a
  specific enabled monitor or select **Automatic · app's monitor** to restore
  the default.

Home still opens at startup and remains the source for the weather bar and
background notifications. Browsing another city does not change Home.
Hyprland compatibility and effects ownership checks remain in place.

Monitor routing is covered by controlled tests; physical multi-monitor behavior
and live compositor activation were not exercised for this patch.
