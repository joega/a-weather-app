# A Weather App 0.62.2

This release adds saved visual-quality settings and shows forecasts promptly
while official US alerts load independently.

- Settings now offers Auto, Full, Economical and Static window visuals. Auto
  preserves Full on supported hardware graphics and uses Static on software
  graphics, including llvmpipe and Qt raster. An unsupported or failed shader
  pipeline stays Static even when Full is selected. Reduced motion remains
  separate and takes priority.
- Economical updates the sky at 10 FPS and uses fewer wind trails at 10 updates
  per second. Full keeps the existing appearance and wind drawing. Existing
  hidden, minimized and offscreen animation pauses remain in place.
- Validated forecasts appear as soon as available while US alerts continue
  loading. Pending, unavailable and stale cached alert states are labeled
  honestly. Cached alerts remain location-bound, and canceled or superseded
  requests cannot apply alerts to a different location.
- In local private fixtures, forecasts published in 56–58 ms with 1.5-second or
  ten-second alert delays. This measures service publication, not UI display
  latency. Economical wind reduced isolated CPU use by about 47% on the
  development laptop through lower cadence and trail density.

Go/Qt regression tests and rendered clear, rain, snow and thunderstorm checks
passed, including DPR 1/2 and software fallback. Older hardware compatibility,
whole-app battery savings and other operating systems have not been measured.
Startup, graphics-resource release and drawing-batching prototypes were not
retained because their measurements did not justify the changes.

The release pipeline retains vulnerability assessments, artifact integrity
checks and independent reproducibility checks. Qt, GTK and graphics libraries
are supplied by the host system; native assessment coverage remains incomplete.
