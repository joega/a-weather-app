# A Weather App 0.70.0

This feature release brings a cleaner forecast screen, a saved-location sidebar,
observed radar and richer weather details to the native Linux app.

- **A calmer forecast screen.** Centered current conditions, a compact icon
  toolbar and restored hourly temperature/precipitation bars keep the weather
  easy to scan. Dry daytime clouds have a brighter sky, and the background sun
  and moon stay clear of the main text. A back-to-top button helps with longer
  dashboards.
- **Saved locations with a Home.** Home opens at startup, stays pinned first in
  the sidebar, and continues to control the weather bar, desktop effects and
  notifications while you browse other cities. One automatically saved list
  supports inline editing, ordering, removal and adding places. Shorter city and
  region names, lightweight current-condition updates and accurate alert counts
  make the cards more useful.
- **Observed radar, ready when you need it.** Radar is the first map tab and
  opens paused on the newest NOAA/NWS observation. Drag to pan, zoom, recenter or
  expand the map; choose Play or browse the timeline to load recent history.
  Clearer background tiles and steadier image transitions improve reading and
  playback. Forecast precipitation, temperature and wind remain separate tabs,
  with hour markers and local timestamps on their timeline.
- **Clearer alerts and opt-in official warning notifications.** Active alerts
  use two columns when space permits, with expandable instructions. Separate
  US warning notifications offer severity preferences, quiet hours, an urgent
  override and a temporary delivery pause. Notification actions and recent
  notices open the original warning for its original place; monitoring follows
  Home even while you view another city.
- **More useful weather details.** Select an hour, day or metric card for
  keyboard-navigable charts and textual values. Sun & Moon opens on today with
  sunrise, sunset, daylight and lunar information, plus optional date changes.
  Daily details offer modeled rain and snow amounts. Air quality distinguishes
  the US and European index categories and adds a 48-hour pollutant outlook.
- **Make the dashboard yours.** Settings offers section and metric ordering,
  visibility, density and hourly display preferences, plus 100%, 125% and 150%
  text sizes and high contrast. These choices persist across restarts.
- **Experimental tools have their own place.** Outdoor planner, Forecast changes
  and Share forecast now live at the bottom of Settings under Experimental.
  Sharing supports text and PNG export, readable named-month dates, day-period
  summaries and an option to omit the place name. These tools remain available
  for evaluation without crowding the main forecast.

Optional maps and detail panels are constructed on demand. Only the selected
map renders; offscreen or hidden views stop their map work. Radar history waits
for explicit playback or browsing, and saved-location summaries use bounded
current-condition requests rather than loading a full forecast for every city.
Repeated-use checks cover cleanup and bounded caches; this is not a battery-life
or low-end hardware certification.

Observed radar and official alerts/warnings cover the contiguous United States
and supported US locations respectively. Radar shows past observations, not
minute-by-minute rain predictions. Rain/snow and air-quality outlooks are modeled
data. Warning delivery can be delayed and is not a real-time emergency service.
The outdoor planner does not account for every hazard.

Existing saved locations migrate to the single-list format with the primary
place becoming Home. Legacy files are retained for rollback; back up the state
directory before downgrading because later edits are not mirrored into those
older files.

The app remains Go plus C++/Qt Quick/QML. Desktop effects retain the existing
Hyprland compatibility checks, and the release pipeline retains artifact
integrity, vulnerability assessment and independent reproducibility gates.
The release compiler advances to Go 1.27.2 to include the October security fixes.
Host Qt, GTK and graphics libraries remain system dependencies; native security
assessment and broader platform/accessibility coverage remain incomplete.
